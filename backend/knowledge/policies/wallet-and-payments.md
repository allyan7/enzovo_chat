# Kartly Wallet and Payments Policy

Effective: 1 July 2026.

## 1. Kartly Wallet basics

- The wallet holds two kinds of balance:
  - **Cash balance**: money added by the customer, refunds and cashback. Does not expire.
  - **Promo credit**: promotional credit from Kartly. Expires on the date shown, can pay for at most 10% of an order's value, and cannot be withdrawn.
- Balance limits: ₹10,000 for minimum-KYC accounts, ₹2,00,000 for full-KYC accounts.
- Money can be added by UPI, card or net banking.

## 2. Paying with the wallet

- The wallet can pay for the whole order or part of it. The rest can be paid by UPI, card, net banking or COD (split payment).
- If the wallet balance is lower than the order total, the customer is offered a split payment; the order is not placed until the full amount is covered.
- The wallet debit and the order creation happen together. If the order fails to be created, the debit is reversed automatically within 15 minutes.
- When ordering through the support chatbot, the customer must see the item, the total, the amount taken from the wallet and the balance left, and confirm before the order is placed.

## 3. Cashback

- Gold members get 1% cashback on mobiles, up to ₹450 per order.
- Cashback is credited to the wallet's cash balance 7 days after delivery (once the return window closes).
- Cashback is reversed if the item is returned.

## 4. Payment failed but money was debited

Sometimes the bank debits the money but the payment does not reach Kartly (for example, a timeout). In this case:
- No order is created.
- Kartly's payment gateway starts an automatic reversal right away.
- The bank returns the money within **5 business days**. The expected date is shown in the order details along with the bank reference number (RRN).
- If it has not arrived by then, the customer can contact support with the RRN.

## 5. Charged twice

If the same order is charged twice, the duplicate charge is detected within 24 hours and refunded automatically to the original method, using the timelines in the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 5.

## 6. EMI

No-cost EMI for 3 or 6 months is available on orders of ₹10,000 or more with selected credit cards.

## 7. Withdrawing wallet balance

Full-KYC customers can transfer their cash balance to a verified bank account; it arrives in 2 business days. Promo credit cannot be withdrawn.
