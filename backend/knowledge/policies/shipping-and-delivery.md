# enzovo Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective: 1 July 2026.

## 1. Delivery charges

- Free delivery on orders of ₹499 or more.
- ₹49 delivery fee below ₹499.

## 2. Dispatch and delivery time

- Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day; later orders the next day.
- Delivery time depends on the pincode (typically 2 days in metro cities, up to 8 days for remote areas). The expected delivery date is shown at checkout and in order details.

## 3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available on eligible pincodes for orders up to ₹50,000.

## 4. Changing the address or delivery slot

- The delivery address can be changed only **before dispatch**, and only to another serviceable pincode.
- After dispatch, the customer can ask the courier to reschedule the delivery date (up to 3 days later) but not change the address.

## 5. Secure delivery

Orders worth ₹10,000 or more need a one-time password (OTP) shared with the delivery agent at handover.

## 6. Delays

Shipments can be delayed by weather, local restrictions, or high volume. When a delay happens:
- The order shows a **revised expected delivery date** and the reason.
- The customer is notified by SMS and in the app.
- If the shipment is not delivered within 7 days of the original expected date, the customer can cancel for a full refund (Cancellation Policy, section 3).

## 7. Gift orders

Gift orders can carry a message. Prices are not printed on the packing slip.
