# enzovo Cancellation Policy

Effective: 1 July 2026.

## 1. Before dispatch — free cancellation

Orders can be cancelled at no charge while the status is **placed**, **confirmed** or **packed** (that is, before the shipment is dispatched).

- Prepaid orders are refunded as per the Returns and Refunds Policy, sections 4 and 5.
- Any wallet amount used goes back to the wallet within 2 hours.
- COD orders: nothing to refund.

## 2. After dispatch

Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. The customer can:
- **Refuse the delivery** at the door. The refund starts once the parcel is back at the warehouse, usually 5–7 days later.
- Or accept it and use the return or replacement options, if the category allows.

## 3. Delivery delayed by more than 7 days

If a shipment has not been delivered **7 days after its original expected delivery date**, the customer may cancel it while in transit and get a full refund, regardless of the dispatch status.

## 4. Partial cancellation

Individual items in a multi-item order can be cancelled before dispatch. Coupon discounts are split across items as described in the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 7.

## 5. Cancellations by enzovo

enzovo may cancel an order if the item goes out of stock, the address is not serviceable, or the payment is flagged as suspicious. The customer receives a full refund to the original method.

## 6. Cancelling through the support chatbot

The chatbot can cancel an eligible order on the customer's behalf. It must first show the order, the amount and where the refund will go, and wait for the customer to confirm.
